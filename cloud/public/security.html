Check where you're signed in

Yap lists every device signed in to your account, and can sign any of them out.

  1. Open Yap on your PC.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Under Where you're signed in, sign out any device you don't recognise.
Open Yap

Your Yap account holds only your name, email and sign-ins. Your dictation, notes and settings stay on your PC. A sign-in approved with a phone can't delete your account or sign out your other devices during its first day, so you can always take it back.
