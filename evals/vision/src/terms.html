Riverside Climbing Wall · Members' area

Terms of membership (version 4, 1 September 2026)

1. Who these terms are for

These terms apply to everyone who holds an adult, student or family membership at Riverside Climbing Wall, 3 Mill Lane, Exampleton. A junior member's parent or guardian accepts them on the junior's behalf. Day-pass visitors are covered by the separate visitor terms shown at reception.

2. Induction

Before climbing unsupervised, every member must complete a 45-minute induction with one of our instructors. Members who already hold a recognised belay certificate may take the shorter 15-minute check instead. We may ask you to repeat the induction if we see unsafe belaying.

3. Membership fees

Adult membership is £42 a month and student membership £34 a month, paid by direct debit on the 1st of each month. Family membership covers two adults and up to three juniors for £96 a month. Prices may change once a year; we will tell you at least 28 days before a change takes effect.

4. Your membership card

Your card is personal to you and must be shown at reception on every visit. A lost or damaged card can be replaced at reception for £5. Lending your card to someone else may lead to your membership being suspended.

5. Freezing your membership

You may freeze your membership for between one and three months in any 12-month period, for injury, travel or study, at £6 a month while frozen. Ask at reception or email members@riverside.example.com at least 7 days before the freeze should start.

6. Cancelling

You may cancel at any time by giving us 30 days' notice in writing, by email or using the form at reception. Your last payment will be taken at the end of the notice period. Memberships in their first three months (the minimum term) can be cancelled only from the end of the third month, unless you move more than 20 miles away.

7. Guests

Adult members may bring one guest a month at the member guest rate of £9. Guests must complete the visitor form and a short safety briefing before climbing.

8. Opening hours

We are open 10:00 to 22:00 on weekdays and 09:00 to 20:00 at weekends. The bouldering room closes for resetting every second Tuesday from 10:00 to 16:00; the dates are on the noticeboard and in the app.

9. Lockers and belongings

Day lockers take a £1 coin, which is returned. Items left overnight are moved to lost property and kept for 14 days. We are not responsible for valuables left outside a locker.

10. Behaviour

Please keep to the marked routes, never climb above another climber, and keep the matted areas clear. Staff may ask anyone behaving unsafely to stop climbing for the rest of the day.

11. Contact

Questions about your membership: members@riverside.example.com or 07700 900213 (Monday to Friday, 10:00 to 17:00).
